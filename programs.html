Programs shaped to match your security maturity.

SecuryHub manages the entire lifecycle of your bug bounty program—from onboarding to researcher communications—so your teams can focus on remediation.

Two paths to engage researchers

Choose an open program to build brand trust or reserve access for an invite-only roster. We guide you to the right mix.

Public program

Visibility for your organisation with a published policy and a monitored disclosure channel. Reputation-based rewards keep quality high while giving new researchers a meaningful way to contribute.

Private program

Invite only the researchers who match your technology stack. We manage NDAs, briefing sessions, and ongoing calibrations to ensure a consistent signal-to-noise ratio.

What we handle for every engagement

Your SecuryHub team takes ownership of the operational tasks that keep programs effective.

Policy authoring

We translate your internal security requirements into a clear, researcher-friendly policy that outlines scope, rules of engagement, and safe harbor language.

Researcher enablement

Briefings, environment access, and escalation paths are coordinated by our community team so researchers can focus on responsible testing.

Lifecycle management

From first response to fix verification, we document every step, track service-level expectations, and prepare leadership-ready summaries.

Decision support for security leaders

We understand that budget holders need clear context to evaluate investment. Each quarter you receive a detailed review covering program health, vulnerability trends, and recommended adjustments to scope or incentives.

Our analysts maintain a shared backlog with your engineering owners, ensuring no validated issue loses visibility.

Quarterly business review highlights

  • Key metrics with year-over-year comparisons
  • Detailed discussion of high-impact findings
  • Researcher spotlight and access changes
  • Forward-looking roadmap suggestions
