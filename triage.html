AI-supported triage with human accountability

The SecuryHub AI analyst accelerates review without compromising judgment. Automation handles repetitive checks while our security specialists make final decisions.

Duplicate detection

The AI analyst correlates new submissions against historical findings, matching payloads, affected assets, and impact summaries.

Scope alignment

Reports are screened against program policies to ensure the asset and vulnerability type qualify before analysts invest further time.

Guided validation

Where safe, the AI analyst executes scripted checks or browser automations to verify issues such as SQLi, XXE, XSS, SSRF, and critical API flaws.

Triage workflow

  1. Intake — Reports arrive through encrypted channels and are acknowledged within two business days.
  2. Automated review — The AI analyst checks for duplicates, validates scope, and proposes severity.
  3. Human validation — SecuryHub analysts reproduce the issue, consult subject matter experts, and craft remediation notes.
  4. Resolution — Customers receive detailed findings while researchers see transparent status updates.

Status transitions

  • New — Awaiting automated review.
  • In review — AI analyst completed checks; human validation in progress.
  • Triaged — Issue confirmed with clear reproduction and impact.
  • Resolved — Fix verified and researcher acknowledged.

When automation escalates

If the AI analyst cannot validate a reported bug using the provided proof-of-concept, the submission remains marked “In review.” Our human team steps in to perform manual testing before any status changes occur.

This approach ensures researchers receive timely feedback while maintaining the accuracy customers expect.

Transparency for customers

Dashboards show how many reports were automatically validated, which ones require analyst follow-up, and the median time spent in each stage. Customers can export evidence for audits or compliance reports on demand.
