Private programs for qualified researchers.

SecuryHub private programs are available to researchers who have demonstrated consistent, high-quality results. Invitations prioritise community members with strong reputations in our public initiatives.

Access policy

Private programs provide access to pre-release environments, sensitive assets, and coordinated testing calendars. To preserve customer trust, access is limited to researchers who have passed identity verification, legal checks, and performance reviews.

Qualified researchers receive scenario briefings, responsible disclosure expectations, and direct channels to SecuryHub analysts.

Request access

Researchers interested in private programs can contact our Capture the Flag (CTF) team. Please include recent achievements, areas of technical focus, and references.

Email: ctf@securyhub.com

Expectations for private engagements

These standards ensure a productive collaboration between researchers and our customers.

Structured testing windows

Private programs operate in defined sprints. Attendance in kickoff briefings and timely updates are required.

Expanded asset coverage

Researchers gain access to unreleased products, internal tooling, and staged infrastructure with detailed documentation.

Recognition & rewards

Invited researchers receive elevated reputation weighting, priority review in future engagements, and tailored thank-you packages from participating customers.
