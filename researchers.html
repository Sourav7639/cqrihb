How to get started

We welcome researchers of all backgrounds who demonstrate professionalism and respect for coordinated disclosure.

  1. Review the SecuryHub policy to understand scope expectations.
  2. Submit thoughtful findings through the disclosure email with clear reproduction details.
  3. Earn reputation points for valid reports and constructive collaboration.
  4. Receive invitations to private programs as your reputation grows.

Conduct guidelines

  • Communicate respectfully with customers and fellow researchers.
  • Share reproduction steps and impact analysis to help teams remediate quickly.
  • Honor embargoes and confidentiality requirements.
  • Keep testing within defined windows and scopes.

Tools and resources

Our analyst team publishes regular guidance on testing methodologies, tooling, and disclosure best practices. These resources are available to active community members.

  • Proof-of-concept clinics led by senior researchers
